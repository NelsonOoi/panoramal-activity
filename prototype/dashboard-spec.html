Dashboard spec
Prototype with sample data. Visual style: shadcn/ui dark (zinc), one primary action, outline badges, no per-camera colours.

Cameras

  • Camera grid: 4 cameras per page, most active first (alert → people in view → live → guest → offline). Left and right buttons page through the rest ("1–4 of 12"). Order changes only when something happens (an alert, a camera joining), never every frame.
  • Each tile shows the latest frame with boxes, a status badge, an Alert tag if one is open, the observation count, who's in view and the last 6 frames.
  • Selecting a tile opens the camera view. The observation log lives there, not on the main screen.
  • No floor plan. "Learned handoffs" lists the links learned from people moving between cameras (average time, count).
  • QR cards: add a camera (/capture) and "Saw something?" (/witness).

Camera view (select a tile)

  • The latest frame, large, with each person's box and label.
  • In view now (id, description, action), objects, and the latest notable event.
  • Recent log: the camera's memory, newest first. Unchanged runs collapse into one line (“A · standing at the table · 14:02:58–14:03:30 · 32 frames”). Select a row to show that frame.
  • Ask this camera: the question goes to the camera over Relay, and it answers from its own memory with cited frames. Works when the camera is offline.

New incident

  • Question, time window (1 / 5 / 15 min or custom), cameras (all selected). Always starts a fresh case.
  • "What just happened?" is the one-click version: last minute, all cameras.

Alerts

  • Raised when a camera sees a big change: an object gone, an object left behind, a crowd change (±3 people), the camera covered or moved, or a notable event the model flags.
  • Toast (bottom-left): camera, what changed, time, Acknowledge / Investigate. A countdown shows when it will escalate.
  • Escalation is a Terse alarm on the alert. If nobody responds within 30 s, the alert starts an investigation by itself (window 30 s before to 15 s after) and notifies on-call over Relay.
  • Acknowledge cancels the alarm. Investigate opens the case straight away. Each alert's history is kept (raised, acknowledged, escalated).

Case file

  • Question, status, window and witnesses; a storyboard per person (camera to camera, with dashed "unseen" gaps); a timeline with citations (each opens that frame); the contradiction; the verdict.
  • A guest clip that fills a gap makes the dashed connector solid, and changed items are marked Revised with the old claim struck through.
